INNRVZN Seer – Privacy policy

INNRVZN Seer is an internal work tool used by the INNRVZN team to view and update the team's own Google Sheets and Google Drive folders.

What Seer accesses

When you sign in with Google, Seer asks for access to Google Sheets, Google Drive and your email address. It uses them only to read and write the INNRVZN tracker sheets, to create and open project folders and deliverables in the INNRVZN Drive folder, and to show which account is signed in.

Where your data goes

Seer has no server of its own. Data moves directly between your device and Google. Sign-in tokens are stored only on your device and are removed when you sign out. Seer does not sell, share or use your data for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Removing access

Sign out in Seer (More → Sign out), or remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the INNRVZN team through the user support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
